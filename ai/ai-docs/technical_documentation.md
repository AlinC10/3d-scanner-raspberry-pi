# Automated 3D Scanner - Technical Documentation

## 1. Edge-to-Cloud Distributed Architecture
The system implements a hybrid edge-to-cloud Internet of Things (IoT) architecture designed to decouple local acquisition hardware from intensive computational processing.
*   **Edge Orchestrator**: A Raspberry Pi 5 (8 GB RAM, BCM2712 Cortex-A76 @ 2.4 GHz) running Debian Trixie. It hosts a FastAPI REST service, handles the state machine, controls camera configurations (via libcamera/Picamera2), and manages concurrent upload workers to a Cloudflare R2 S3-compatible object storage.
*   **Real-Time Motion MCU**: An Arduino Uno R3 (ATmega328P @ 16 MHz) acts as a dedicated motion co-processor. This is required because Linux scheduling jitter (tens to hundreds of microseconds) is comparable to the motor step period (1 ms), which can excite mechanical resonances and cause missed steps. The Arduino generates cycle-counted delays at a 62.5 ns resolution and communicates with the orchestrator via a 115,200 Baud USB serial link.

## 2. Electro-Mechanical & Kinematic Systems
The scanner utilizes a cylindrical-coordinate manipulator geometry.
*   **Turntable**: Driven by a NEMA 17 stepper motor and a TB6600 driver operating at 12V with 1.5A. Configured for 1/16 microstepping, achieving an angular resolution of 0.1125° per microstep. A standard 18° rotation takes 160 microsteps, equating to an arc resolution of 0.255 mm at the rim of the Ø260 mm turntable.
*   **Z-Axis Carriage**: Driven by a NEMA 17 motor with a T8 lead screw (8 mm lead) and an anti-backlash nut, guided by MGN9 rails. Configured for 1/4 microstepping, yielding a vertical resolution of 0.01 mm. Hardware endstops (GPIO4, GPIO22) with internal pull-ups and a 20 ms debounce filter control homing and absolute limits.
*   **Motion Profile**: The firmware executes a trapezoidal velocity profile. For a single 18° stop, the move lasts 224 ms with a cruise speed of 112.5°/s. An out-of-band 0xFF byte protocol ensures emergency abort latency is bounded to ≤ 4.1 ms.

## 3. Vision and Synchronization
*   **Sensors**: Two Arducam IMX477 12.3 MP cameras (4056x3040, pixel pitch 1.55 µm, focal length f = 3.9 mm) featuring 10-bit Voice Coil Motors (VCM) for motorized focus. They are positioned at a radial distance of 300 mm with an angular separation of 20°.
*   **Resolution and Precision**: The geometric Ground Sampling Distance (GSD) is approximately 0.12 mm/px. Expected depth precision from the convergent pair is σ_Z ≈ 0.17 mm.
*   **Frame Synchronization**: Sub-microsecond frame alignment is achieved via an External Vertical Synchronization (XVS) hardware pad. The slave camera is initialized first and blocks until the master emits vertical-sync pulses.

## 4. Active Illumination and Sensing
*   **LED PWM Control**: 12V LED strips are switched by a relay (GPIO11) and dimmed by a D4184 MOSFET using 10 kHz hardware PWM (GPIO12) generated by the RP1 controller. The high frequency ensures residual PWM ripple is bounded to ≤ 0.5%, preventing rolling-shutter banding.
*   **Zero-Current Switching (ZCS)**: The MCU strictly enforces ZCS. Duty cycle is held at 0 for a 50 ms contact-settling time when the relay opens or closes, preventing contact arcing as the relay never switches under load.
*   **Adaptive ToF Scanning**: A VL53L0X Time-of-Flight sensor (940 nm, I2C) detects object height. The scanner utilizes a debounced boolean logic gate: if two consecutive readings indicate a distance greater than the back wall threshold (535 mm), the scanning loop terminates early.

## 5. Automated Photometric Stabilization
*   **Thermal Gate**: During warmup, the system monitors the exposure-gain product (m(t) = E(t)G(t) ∝ 1/Φ(t)) to infer luminous flux without external sensors. Scanning begins only when thermal drift is < 3% over a 10-second window.
*   **Autofocus (AF)**: A contrast-based AF sweep tests Voice Coil Motor codes across P = {0, 30, ..., 1020}, maximizing the variance of the Laplacian in a central 40% × 40% Region of Interest.

## 6. Cloud Orchestration and Photogrammetry
The photogrammetric pipeline utilizes AliceVision Meshroom deployed on ephemeral RunPod GPUs.
*   **Memory-Aware Sizer**: To mitigate silent Out-of-Memory (OOM) failures, the orchestrator predicts RAM usage with the empirical model: M_req = 1.1(4GB + N × R × K + P / 1.5 × 10^6). Jobs requiring ≤ 64 GB are routed to consumer GPUs, while larger jobs are allocated to high-memory hosts.
*   **Feature Matching**: Employs DSP-SIFT descriptors matched via approximate nearest neighbors, utilizing AC-RANSAC (A Contrario RANSAC) to verify epipolar constraints automatically without fixed pixel tolerances.
*   **Rig-Constrained Bundle Adjustment (BA)**: By explicitly defining the stereo rig via modified EXIF tags, the pose unknowns during incremental SfM drop from 6 × n_c × n_p to 6 × n_p + 6(n_c - 1) (a 49% reduction in parameters), heavily stabilizing BA on weakly textured geometries.
*   **Densification**: Depth maps are computed via Semi-Global Matching (SGM) and downscaled by a factor of 2. The point cloud is converted via Delaunay tetrahedralization, labeled inside/outside using an s-t minimum graph cut, and smoothed via two Laplacian iterations.
*   **Draco Compression**: The final output includes a GLB file compressed with the KHR_draco_mesh_compression extension using 14 quantization bits, ensuring a maximum geometric distortion of ≤ 7.9 µm across a 260 mm bounding box.
